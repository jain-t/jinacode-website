Workday Policy

Hours · Working from home · Onsite work

This page sets out when and where the Jinacode Systems team works. If you are applying to join us, please read it before you confirm on the application form that it works for you.

Working days and hours

  • Monday to Friday.
  • 10:00 am to 7:00 pm IST each working day.
  • At least 45 hours a week.

Working from home

  • Working from home is allowed. Whether it fits depends on the project you are on.
  • Some projects need you onsite, for some days or for a stretch of the project.
  • When you work onsite, the onsite policies of that site apply.

Questions about this policy? Write to developers@jinacode.systems.
