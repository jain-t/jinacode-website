FAQ

The honest answers

How is this different from a staffing agency?

A staffing agency rents you people by the hour and leaves the direction to you. A pod signs up for a result, brings its own lead and way of working, and delivers weekly against an agreed goal.

Do you work directly in our code?

Yes. We use your code, your systems and your tools. Everything we build belongs to you from day one.

What if the scope changes?

It usually does. We handle changes in the open: we re-plan together, adjust the goal, and never bill for surprises you didn't agree to.

How fast can a pod start?

Usually within a week of agreeing the scope. Real code lands in the first week, not after a month of meetings.

How is pricing set?

Pricing depends on the project. Book a call for a fixed quote — a fixed monthly price per pod, with scope changes agreed openly.
