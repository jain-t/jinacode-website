How we work

AI writes code. You own it.

Our principles
01

Claude Code by default

Every engineer uses Claude Code daily to write, review, test and refactor. We care how well you steer it, and how carefully you check what it produces.

02

Close to the problem

You talk to the people who have the problem. No relay of tickets through three layers.

03

Production is the point

A demo isn't done. Tests, monitoring, cost and the unglamorous last stretch are the actual job.

04

Understand what you ship

We're not hiring people who can prompt an AI but can't explain the code it wrote. If you can't walk us through it, it isn't ready.
